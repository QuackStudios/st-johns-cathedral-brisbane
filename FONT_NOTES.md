# Display typography

Display text uses the SangBleu Kingdom family when installed locally. Supplied project assets did not include licensed SangBleu Kingdom webfonts. The current deployed fallback is the system's Iowan Old Style, Palatino, or Georgia, in that order. Do not represent this fallback as SangBleu.

To activate hosted SangBleu, add the licensed Regular and Italic WOFF2 files in dist/fonts and update the corresponding @font-face src declarations in style.css. Swiss Typefaces: https://www.swisstypefaces.com/fonts/sangbleu/

# Editorial sources

Page sources are attached to each article in dist/content.js. They are exposed in each page's Read further & sources section. Current leadership, event details and bookings link to their maintained official sources.

# Footer marks and flags

The Diocese logo comes from the Anglican Church Southern Queensland's own masthead. Flags remain in colour and are not cropped or altered. Australian and Aboriginal flag vectors were sourced from their Wikimedia file pages. The Torres Strait Islander flag's vector representation follows https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Torres_Strait_Islanders.svg; the design is by Bernard Namok, with copyright held by Torres Strait Island Regional Council. The footer uses it as a small identifying symbol alongside the acknowledgement and reconciliation link. Council information: https://tsirc.qld.gov.au/about-us/our-region/torres-strait-islander-flag/

The acknowledgement is original editorial copy and should be reviewed against the Cathedral's adopted wording before a public launch.
