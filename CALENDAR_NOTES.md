Daily panel — implementation and editorial coverage

All date/time decisions use Australia/Brisbane and refresh every 30 seconds, on visibility return, and across midnight. Started services are shaded (not described as finished because durations are not published). The first service whose starting minute has not passed is highlighted.

Timetable source, checked 2 November 2026:
https://www.stjohnscathedral.com.au/services/
The introductory summary says noon Eucharist Monday–Friday, while the detailed SERVICE TIMES specifies Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Friday 7 AM. This implementation follows the detailed list. Saturday has no published regular timetable. Special/festival schedules need dateOverrides in today.js.

Coverage is principal festivals, calculated Easter/seasonal dates and selected fixed commemorations, with a full November selection. This is NOT a complete licensed An Australian Lectionary feed. The public epray site supplies daily prayer but no documented public API was found. Lesser commemorations are optional. Colours are conventional seasonal/observance colours; local vestments can differ. Annual transfers, patronal observances, dedication anniversary, alternate saints dates, Christmas/Easter special services, and Cathedral cancellations require clergy-approved annual overrides. Do not imply live calendar synchronisation. Link to publisher's daily prayer is provided to visitors. Local annual calendar/data feed is needed before claiming complete daily coverage.

Calendar uses primary feast precedence for the Easter octave/Holy Week and Sunday suppression of lesser festivals. It does not calculate every annual APBA transfer. dateOverrides allows explicit approved observance and service arrays keyed YYYY-MM-DD.
